Tools that need your approval

Agents can use every Kitsune tool. A tool checked here waits until you allow it, in the Kitsune toolbar popup or in the agent's own prompt: once, or for the rest of that agent's session. Its use inside Python scripts and batches waits too. By default the tools that read cookies, page storage, the clipboard, saved sessions and history are checked.

History and bookmarks

Lets agents connected to Kitsune search your browsing history and bookmarks. They get URLs, titles, visit times and bookmark folders only, never page contents, form data, cookies or passwords, and only within the Kitsune bridge's history policy (by default the last 30 days).
